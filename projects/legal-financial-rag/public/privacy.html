LexiVault Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 August 2026

In short: LexiVault has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no server of its own. It doesn't save anything to disk at all — documents you ingest, your session's audit log, and the vault key you derive exist only in memory and are gone the moment you close or reload the app.

Who this policy is for

This policy covers the LexiVault mobile and web application, published by Jared Fisher ("we", "us"). It explains what the app does with information you enter.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, and no advertising SDK. It sends no personal data to us or to any third party, because it has no backend service and makes no data-carrying network requests at all.

How your data is handled on your device

LexiVault is a working demonstration of a client-side legal/financial document retrieval tool. Everything you do in it — pasting or ingesting a document, running a query, unlocking the vault, the session's audit trail — exists only in the app's in-memory state while the tab or app is open. Nothing is written to disk: the app does not use localStorage, sessionStorage, or IndexedDB. Closing the tab, reloading the page, or backgrounding and force-closing the Android app clears everything, with no way to recover it. A 5-minute inactivity auto-lock and an unload handler additionally overwrite sensitive buffers (the derived vault key and the last query response) in memory before that point.

A vault passphrase you enter is used locally, via PBKDF2, to derive an AES-GCM key that also never leaves memory. There is no password recovery, because there is nothing to recover it from — we never receive the passphrase or the derived key, and neither is stored anywhere.

Third-party connections

The app has no analytics, no ad networks, and no third-party API calls. One thing reaches the network:

  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) for the app's typography. As with loading a font on any website, that request tells Google your IP address and device user-agent; it carries none of your documents, queries, or audit log. See the Google Privacy Policy.

The app's Content-Security-Policy (connect-src 'self' data: blob:) blocks any script-initiated network request outside the app's own origin, so no document content, query, or audit data can be exfiltrated even by a bug — this is enforced by the browser, not just a policy statement.

Permissions

On Android the app requests only INTERNET, required to load the fonts described above. It requests no location, storage, contacts, or other sensitive permission.

Children

LexiVault is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone. Because the app collects no personal data at all, it holds no children's data.

Keeping and deleting your data

There is nothing to delete on request, because nothing persists in the first place. Closing the app, reloading the page, hitting the 5-minute inactivity auto-lock, or uninstalling the app all have the same effect: everything entered during that session is gone.

Security

Because no data is written to disk, there is nothing at rest for a lost or unlocked device to expose. While the app is open and unlocked, session data lives in memory like any running app's — the auto-lock and unload wipe described above are there to shrink that window, not to protect data that has been saved anywhere.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data handling changes, this policy will be updated and the date at the top revised. Material changes will be reflected in the app's store listing.
