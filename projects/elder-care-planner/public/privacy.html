Elder Care Planner Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Your plan stays local unless you choose to share it. There are no accounts, analytics, or advertising. Calculations run on your device, and we do not receive your plan, facility photos, or receipts. You can choose to export a plan or send an encrypted family snapshot.

Who this policy is for

This policy covers the Elder Care Planner mobile and web application, published by Jared Fisher ("we", "us"). It explains what the app does with information on your device.

Information we collect

We do not collect your plan or personal information through the app. There are no user accounts, sign-in, analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs. The installed Android app makes no network requests for calculations or storage. The web version downloads its application files from its host when you open it; the host may receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address. Your care costs, savings, income, and family details are not included in those requests. Information you choose to email to our support address is received by us and our email provider.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following in your browser's or the app's local storage. We have no access to this storage. Browser settings, operating-system tools, and any exports or shares you create can affect where copies are kept.

Stored itemWhat it contains
elder-care-planner:state:v1 (localStorage) Your planner form: care costs, savings, income, benefit timing, the sibling contribution ledger, facility notes, and other entries. This can include sensitive financial and medical detail you choose to enter.
Facility photos (IndexedDB) Photos you attach to a facility in your shortlist, stored separately from the plan so a large photo library cannot crowd out your plan data.
Receipts (IndexedDB) Photos of receipts you attach to a ledger entry, stored the same way.

Sharing, exports, and external links

Sharing is optional. A family link contains a snapshot of your plan encrypted with the passphrase you choose. The encrypted payload is in the URL fragment (after #), which is not sent to the web host in an HTTP request. Send the passphrase separately. The recipient can read the snapshot with the passphrase; it is not live syncing. Facility shortlist photos and receipt photos are excluded from these snapshots.

Anyone with the link and passphrase can read or forward the snapshot. Messaging services you use may retain the link you send. A downloaded JSON export is unencrypted, and printing or saving a PDF creates another copy. Deleting your local data does not delete copies held by recipients or in exported files.

Resource links open external websites, which have their own privacy policies. The app does not send your plan to those sites. Its calculations use your entries and bundled figures, without analytics or advertising connections.

Permissions

The Android build requests no permissions beyond the bare minimum Android itself requires. Attaching a facility photo or receipt uses the system photo picker, which does not require a storage or camera permission from the app.

Children

Elder Care Planner is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone. Because the app collects no personal data at all, it holds no children's data.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. Because we never receive it, there is nothing for us to delete and no data-deletion request to make. To erase everything the app has stored:

  • Android: Settings → Apps → Elder Care Planner → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.
  • Web: clear site data for the page in your browser's settings.

Security

Normal autosave encrypts the planner form with AES-GCM using a device-bound key held in IndexedDB. This is not a passphrase lock: someone with access to your unlocked app or browser can still view the plan. If the key is unavailable, or the page is closing and a synchronous save is needed, the form can be saved in plaintext so the last edit is not lost. A later successful encrypted save replaces it. Facility photos and receipts are not separately encrypted by the app. Protect your device with a lock screen and keep exported files private.

Android backup rules exclude the app's WebView storage from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Your browser or other device tools may have their own backup behavior. Do not rely on app uninstall to erase exported or shared copies.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data handling changes, this policy will be updated and the date at the top revised. Material changes will be reflected in the app's store listing.
