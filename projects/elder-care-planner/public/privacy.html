In short: Elder Care Planner makes no network requests at all. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. Everything you enter — your plan, facility photos, and receipts — stays on your device and is never transmitted anywhere, including to us.

Who this policy is for

This policy covers the Elder Care Planner mobile and web application, published by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we", "us"). It explains what the app does with information on your device.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information, and cannot. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, no advertising SDK, and makes no outbound network requests of any kind — care costs, savings, income, and family details you enter are financially and medically sensitive, and the app is built so none of it can leave your device even by accident.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following in your browser's or the app's local storage. It stays on your device and is not readable by us.

Stored itemWhat it contains
elder-care-planner:plan:v1 (localStorage) Your full plan: care costs, savings, income, benefit timing, the sibling contribution ledger, and any notes you add. This can include sensitive financial and medical detail you choose to enter.
Facility photos (IndexedDB) Photos you attach to a facility in your shortlist, stored separately from the plan so a large photo library cannot crowd out your plan data.
Receipts (IndexedDB) Photos of receipts you attach to a ledger entry, stored the same way.

Third-party connections

None. The app has no analytics, no ad networks, and makes no network requests of any kind — every calculation runs on your device using data you typed in or figures built into the app itself.

Permissions

The Android build requests no permissions beyond the bare minimum Android itself requires. Attaching a facility photo or receipt uses the system photo picker, which does not require a storage or camera permission from the app.

Children

Elder Care Planner is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone. Because the app collects no personal data at all, it holds no children's data.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. Because we never receive it, there is nothing for us to delete and no data-deletion request to make. To erase everything the app has stored:

  • Android: Settings → Apps → Elder Care Planner → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.
  • Web: clear site data for the page in your browser's settings.

Security

Because the app stores data only on your device and transmits none of it, the main protection is your device's own security — its lock screen and app sandboxing. Data stored locally is not encrypted separately by the app, so anyone with access to an unlocked device could open the app and view your plan, photos, and receipts.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data handling changes, this policy will be updated and the date at the top revised. Material changes will be reflected in the app's store listing.
