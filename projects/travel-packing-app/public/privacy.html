PackRight Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 August 2026

In short: PackRight has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. Your wardrobe photos, packing lists, and trip details stay on your device. Two outbound requests are made only when you enter a trip destination — to look up its location and weather — described below.

Who this policy is for

This policy covers the PackRight mobile and web application, published by Jared Fisher ("we", "us"). It explains what the app does with information on your device and what it sends over the network.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, and no advertising SDK. It sends no personal data to us, because it has no backend service of its own.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following in your browser's or the app's local storage (localStorage and IndexedDB). It stays on your device, is not transmitted anywhere, and is not readable by us.

Stored itemWhat it contains
Wardrobe items (IndexedDB) Photos of clothing you add to your digital closet, and the background-removed versions the app generates. Background removal runs entirely on your device (ONNX Runtime Web) — the original photo is never uploaded anywhere.
Trips and packing lists (IndexedDB) Trip destinations, dates, generated outfit schedules, and your packing checklist.
packright_theme, packright_checklist (localStorage) Your light/dark theme choice and packing-checklist check-off state.

Third-party connections

The app has no analytics and no ad networks. Two things reach the network, only when you enter or change a trip destination:

  • Location lookup sends the destination text you typed to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service (nominatim.openstreetmap.org) to resolve it to a coordinate. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.
  • Weather forecast sends that coordinate and your trip dates to Open-Meteo (geocoding-api.open-meteo.com) to build the trip's daily warmth targets. See the Open-Meteo privacy policy.

Neither request carries your wardrobe photos, packing list, or any other stored data — only the destination text and dates you enter for that lookup.

Permissions

On Android the app requests INTERNET, required for the location and weather lookups above, and CAMERA, used only by the suitcase scanner's "📷 Scan" button. That camera stream is read on-device to detect a barcode, or — for the tap-to-measure flow — to capture a single still frame held only in memory so you can tap two points on it against a credit card for scale. Nothing from the camera is written to storage or sent anywhere, in either mode; the still frame is discarded and the camera released the moment the scanner closes. Adding a wardrobe photo is separate and uses the system photo picker, which does not require the camera permission.

Children

PackRight is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone. Because the app collects no personal data at all, it holds no children's data.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. Because we never receive it, there is nothing for us to delete and no data-deletion request to make. To erase everything the app has stored:

  • Android: Settings → Apps → PackRight → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.
  • Web: clear site data for the page in your browser's settings.

Security

Because the app stores data only on your device and transmits none of it (aside from the destination lookups above), the main protection is your device's own security — its lock screen and app sandboxing. Data stored locally is not encrypted separately by the app, so anyone with access to an unlocked device could open the app and view your wardrobe photos and packing lists.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data handling changes, this policy will be updated and the date at the top revised. Material changes will be reflected in the app's store listing.
