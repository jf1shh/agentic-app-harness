MoodDiner Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 August 2026

In short: MoodDiner has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no server of its own. Everything you enter — your saved reservations and any restaurants you add — stays on your device. We never receive it.

Who this policy is for

This policy covers the MoodDiner mobile and web application, published by Jared Fisher (“we”, “us”). It explains what the app does with information on your device.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, and no advertising SDK. It does not request your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files. It sends no personal data to us or to any third party, because it has no backend service.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following in your browser’s or the app’s local storage. It stays on your device, is not transmitted anywhere, and is not readable by us.

Stored itemWhat it contains
mood_diner_reservations Reservations you create in the app: the restaurant, date, time, party size, occasion, seating preference, status, and any free-text “special requests” you type. Note that the special-requests box is free text — if you enter something sensitive there, such as an allergy or dietary restriction, it is stored on your device along with the rest of the reservation.
mood_diner_custom_restaurants Details of any restaurant you add yourself, such as its name, address, and website.
Plan and usage counters Your selected plan tier and a daily recommendation-credit count, used only to run the app’s free-tier limits locally. No payment details are collected or stored; the app does not process payments.

Reservations made in MoodDiner are recorded locally for your own reference. The app does not contact restaurants and does not make real bookings on your behalf.

Third-party connections

The app has no analytics and no ad networks. Two things do reach the network:

  • Restaurant images are loaded from Unsplash (images.unsplash.com). As with loading any image on the internet, that request tells Unsplash your IP address, your device’s user-agent, and which image was requested. It carries none of your reservations or other stored data. See the Unsplash privacy policy.
  • Restaurant websites open only when you choose to tap a link. Once you leave the app, the privacy policy of that website applies.

Permissions

On Android the app requests only INTERNET, which is required to load the restaurant images described above. It requests no location, storage, contacts, or other sensitive permission.

Children

MoodDiner is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone. Because the app collects no personal data at all, it holds no children’s data.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. Because we never receive it, there is nothing for us to delete and no data-deletion request to make. To erase everything the app has stored:

  • Android: Settings → Apps → MoodDiner → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.
  • Web: clear site data for the page in your browser’s settings.

Security

Because the app stores data only on your device and transmits none of it, the main protection is your device’s own security — its lock screen and app sandboxing. Data stored locally is not encrypted separately by the app, so anyone with access to an unlocked device could open the app and read the reservations saved in it.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data handling changes, this policy will be updated and the date at the top revised. Material changes will be reflected in the app’s store listing.
